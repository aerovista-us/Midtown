VariationFrontage-first motor court

structure boxes fitturning not provenshared drivesimple construction
What the math changed

The initial “two rear garages across the lot” idea consumes roughly 44′ before setbacks. The geometry-respecting version pushes garage sequencing down the long axis instead.

Strength

Clear construction logic, efficient use of depth, and potentially the lowest structural complexity of the shortlist.

Watch-out

Do not advance it based on a pretty motor-court drawing. It needs a real pickup/SUV swept-path template.
