Option A
Side Drive +
Rear Garage

Clean street face; garage pushed deep into the lot.

Best curb appeal without a front garage door.

Why it works

Strongest Pennsylvania-facing house presence. Preserves a clear front porch and uses the lot's long depth to reach a rear garage.

Watch-out

More driveway and paving. Detached or rear garage placement may increase construction cost and needs access/fire review.

Best use

Best premium feel + street presence

Option B
L-Shaped
House

Program bends with the irregular south boundary.

Most efficient way to tuck the garage and patio.

Why it works

Integrated garage keeps construction straightforward. The L shape creates a protected rear patio and takes advantage of the widening lot.

Watch-out

Needs careful window placement on the 5-foot side and a clean roof-line study.

Best use

Best balanced cost + livability

Option C
Front Courtyard +
Side Garage

Courtyard entry with a tucked garage mass.

Most architectural, but verify turning geometry early.

Why it works

The courtyard makes the narrow frontage feel intentional and gives the Pennsylvania elevation a stronger pedestrian character.

Watch-out

Drive and garage maneuvering need real vehicle turning templates. The courtyard also spends some of the front setback area.

Best use

Best boutique / high-design product
