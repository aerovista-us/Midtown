Watch-out

Garage maneuvering needs real turning templates.

1

Long-axis V2

Two wings open westward down the 148′ depth, using the widening rear of the parcel.
Best architecture, privacy, resale character, and use of the parcel shape
Why it leads

The V finally uses the irregular lot as a design advantage instead of forcing two boxes into a row.

Program

Mathematical test uses ~750 sf first floor + ~1,050 sf second floor per unit, with 22′ × 22′ garages.

Watch-out

Vehicle swept path and the extra cost of angled framing/roof geometry still need proof.

2

E3 Courtyard / Tandem

Two homes separated by an intentional landscaped pause.
Safest strong alternative · easier to understand and likely simpler to build
Fit

Promising measured concept with straightforward rectangular building masses.

Strength

Creates real architectural identity for the rear residence without much structural complexity.

Watch-out

Rear-home identity, separation, and shared access still need development.

3

D2 Motor-Court / Tandem

Practical depth-first arrangement with shared circulation and rear garages.
Efficient and practical, but less special
Fit

Promising geometric fit using simple 20′ × 45′ residential test boxes.

Strength

Efficient construction logic and understandable circulation.

Watch-out

The motor court and turning geometry must work without consuming too much usable yard.

4

E4 Two Detached Homes

Separate structures with stronger single-family identity.
Attractive if ownership / zoning strongly favors detached homes
Fit

Promising physical arrangement using the same measured 900 sf test footprints.

Strength

Best separation and strongest distinct-home character.

Watch-out

Circulation gets heavier, and building separation, fire access, utilities, and ownership structure may control feasibility.
